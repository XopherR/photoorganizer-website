These terms describe basic expectations for using the SnapAtlas website and related guides.

## Use at your own pace

SnapAtlas documentation is meant to help you organize local photo and video libraries more safely. You are responsible for reviewing proposed changes before applying them to your files.

## Recommended precautions

- Start with a small folder before running broad cleanup.
- Use **What-If mode** before major operations.
- Keep backups before reorganization, duplicate cleanup, or permanent deletion.

## Support content

Guides, FAQs, and troubleshooting notes are provided for general help. They may not cover every library layout, metadata condition, or device-specific edge case.

## No guarantee of fit for every library

Photo archives vary widely. Mixed imports, missing metadata, edited copies, and unusual folder structures may require manual review.

## External resources

This site may link to third-party resources. Their content and policies are outside SnapAtlas control.

## Changes

These terms may change as product guidance evolves. Continued use of the site means you accept the latest version posted here.
