---
title: SnapAtlas Photo Organizer
description: A simple guide to what SnapAtlas does and how to use it.
---

SnapAtlas Photo Organizer helps you clean up and organize large photo and video libraries on your computer.

It can:

- Scan and index your folders
- Read photo metadata (date, camera, location)
- Find duplicates (exact copies and look-alikes)
- Organize files into date/location folders
- Merge related folders
- Convert HEIC to JPG
- Stage removals safely before permanent deletion

If your photo collection feels messy, SnapAtlas acts like a smart librarian for your files.
