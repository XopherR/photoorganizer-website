---
title: Who It’s For
description: See whether SnapAtlas is a fit for your photo organization needs.
---

SnapAtlas Photo Organizer is for people who manage large, messy photo libraries across phones, cameras, and backups.

It is especially useful for:

- Families preserving years of memories
- Hobby photographers with mixed camera rolls
- Creators managing repeated shots and edits
- Small studios cleaning up project archives
- Anyone who wants safer cleanup without scripting

If your folders are inconsistent and duplicates are hard to review, SnapAtlas is designed for that exact workflow.
