Duplicate cleanup works best when you split obvious cases from ambiguous ones.

## Recommended steps

1. Run duplicate detection
2. Review **exact duplicates** first
3. Review **visual duplicates** second
4. Move removals to the staging folder
5. Verify staged files before permanent deletion

## Why exact duplicates come first

Exact duplicates are your highest-confidence cases. Resolve them before tuning broader visual matching.

## Tip

If visual results feel too broad or too strict, adjust the similarity threshold and re-run a smaller scope before expanding.
